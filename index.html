About my research

I work at the intersection of environmental data, spatial analysis, and artificial intelligence to investigate coastal rewilding potential across Europe. I use unsupervised learning algorithm to observe the trajectories of coastal landscapes and where they may recover, in present day and in response to future climate extremes.

Open Science & Data Practices

I am also interested in reproducible workflows, good documentation and data interoperability for both humans and machines. As a former Data Steward, I try to make my research as FAIR as possible.
